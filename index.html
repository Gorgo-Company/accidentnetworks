          Everything you need to know about getting help after a car accident in your area.

          Is the helpline really free in your area?

          Yes, 100% free. Our car accident helpline costs you nothing. We connect you with verified attorneys and chiropractors in your county at zero out-of-pocket cost. Attorneys work on contingency — they only get paid if you win.

          How quickly can I see a doctor after my accident?

          Most of our local medical partners can get you an appointment within 24 hours, and many offer same-day availability. Early treatment is critical for your health and for documentation in your case.

          What is the statute of limitations in your state?

          Your state has a statute of limitations of limited time for personal injury claims. Don't wait — evidence disappears, witnesses forget details, and missing the deadline can bar you from recovery entirely. Call us today.

          What if the accident wasn't my fault?

          If another driver caused your accident, you may be entitled to compensation for injuries, lost wages, and pain and suffering. Your state follows comparative fault rules. Our attorneys specialize in proving liability and maximizing your settlement.

          What types of accidents do you cover?

          We assist with car accidents, truck accidents, motorcycle crashes, rideshare accidents, pedestrian injuries, hit-and-run accidents, and more — anywhere in your area or your county. If you were injured, call us.

          Do you serve areas outside of the city?

          Absolutely. While we specialize in the city, our network extends throughout the county and all surrounding communities. Wherever you had your accident in the region, we can help.
